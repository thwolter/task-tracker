Clear, not complicated

Choose, begin,
capture, review.

Tempo supports a deliberate routine for personal work. It is not a tool for surveillance, invoicing, teams, or project planning.

01

Projects that stay clear

Create, rename, or archive projects. Archived projects leave Home while their recorded time remains available in Evaluation.

02

One focused session

Start, pause, resume, or finish a session. Use the tray to switch to another project when necessary.

03

What did you get done?

Capture a short activity note after a session. Correct the start and finish time before saving if needed.

04

Review time with context

See time by day, week, month, or year. Open a project to edit notes or delete individual completed entries.

05

Use your data elsewhere

Export the current evaluation or one project as CSV, Markdown, or JSON.

06

Native, not intrusive

Tempo follows your system light or dark appearance. It uses German for German system locales and English otherwise.

Reviews that stay useful

From focus to a clear record.

Tempo keeps more than a number. A session belongs to a project, receives a short note, and can be reviewed later. Export exactly the evaluation you need.

  • Review all projects or a single project
  • CSV for spreadsheets, Markdown for readable reports, JSON for your own processing
  • On macOS: SQLite backup and restore from the native menu
⌁

Your data remains yours

Stored locally.
Not in the cloud.

Projects, sessions, and notes remain in a local SQLite database on your computer. Tempo needs no sign-in and sends no work data to an external service.
