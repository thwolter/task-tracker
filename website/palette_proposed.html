Text, borders, and focus

The text steps move from dark ink to supporting copy to muted detail. Darker borders outline controls; lighter borders separate content without becoming decorative stripes.

Decisions behind this proposal

The same hues may serve more than one semantic role when the meaning is compatible. The preview removes role names that do not help users of the theme choose a color.

CurrentProposedReason
surface-chrome + surface-softsurface-input #EDF5EBBoth are editable fields; one role gives them the same affordance.

content-secondary #637D66#526654Normal supporting text becomes easier to read.
